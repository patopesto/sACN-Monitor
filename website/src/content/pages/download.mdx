## Platform requirements

- **macOS**: Apple Silicon or Intel, macOS 12 or later. The `.dmg` is signed and notarized.
- **Windows**: Windows 10 or later, amd64 or arm64.
- **Linux**: amd64 or arm64, as AppImage, `.deb`, `.rpm` or an Arch package.

## Homebrew

On macOS you can install and update with Homebrew:

```bash
brew install patopesto/tap/sacn-monitor
```
