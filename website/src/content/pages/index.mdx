## How it works

sACN Monitor listens passively. It never sends DMX, so it can sit on a show network without disturbing it.

For sACN, the app joins the standard universe discovery group and subscribes to every universe a source
advertises, as described in ANSI E1.31. Universes a source does not advertise can be added by hand.

For ArtNet, the app listens to port 6454 on the selected interface and decodes every ArtDMX packet it sees,
whether it was sent to a unicast address, a multicast group or the broadcast address.

The [protocols page](/docs/protocols/) covers the differences in more detail.
