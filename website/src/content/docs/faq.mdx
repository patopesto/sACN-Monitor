## What is sACN?

sACN, or Streaming ACN, carries DMX512-A packets over UDP on an IP network. Each universe is sent to its
own multicast group. It is defined by the [ANSI E1.31](https://tsp.esta.org/tsp/documents/docs/ANSI_E1-31-2018.pdf)
standard.

## What is ArtNet?

ArtNet is a royalty-free protocol from Artistic Licence that also carries DMX512-A over UDP, normally on
port 6454. It uses a Net and SubUni addressing scheme instead of a single universe number. The
[Art-Net 4 specification](https://artisticlicence.com/WebSiteMaster/User%20Guides/art-net.pdf) is
available from Artistic Licence.

## A universe does not show up, but a source is sending it

The app uses the sACN universe discovery mechanism to find universes. Any source that follows the standard advertises its universes, but not every implementation does. Add the universe manually with the **+** button in the Universes column, or with `Cmd/Ctrl+U`.

Before that, check the protocol filter and the selected interface. A console sending ArtNet will not appear while the filter is set to sACN.

## Why are there two rows for the same universe?

The list is keyed by sender as well as universe. Two consoles sending the same universe produce two rows, which lets you detect this collision and compare their data side-by-side.

## Why did the list clear when I changed interfaces?

Switching interfaces restarts both receivers on the new network card and resets the universe list.  
Universes return as packets arrive. Manual sACN adds are re-joined automatically.

## Why is there no priority on an ArtNet universe?

Priority and sync address are sACN fields. Art-Net has no equivalent, so the app hides those rows for ArtNet universes.

## The app cannot see any traffic on macOS

Allow **Local Network** access for sACN Monitor under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.  
Without it macOS blocks the multicast and broadcast traffic the app depends on.

## Does the app send DMX?

No. sACN Monitor is receive-only. It never transmits on the show network.

## Which platforms are supported?

macOS 12 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10 or later (amd64 and arm64), and Linux (amd64 and
arm64). See the [download page](/download/).
