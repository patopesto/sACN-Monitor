## One row per source

The universe list is keyed by protocol, universe number and sender address. If two consoles send the
same universe, the list shows two rows with different sender addresses and lets you inspect each stream
separately. In **Both** mode every row carries an sACN or ArtNet badge.

Universes are sorted by number. The sender address is the IP the packets came from, not the configured
source name.

## sACN discovery

sACN sources announce the universes they transmit on the standard universe discovery group. sACN Monitor
joins that group, subscribes to every advertised universe, and then receives the DMX data on the
universe's own multicast address.

Discovery is part of ANSI E1.31, but not every implementation sends it. If a source is transmitting and
nothing appears, add the universe manually.

## Add an sACN universe manually

Click the **+** button in the bottom right corner of the Universes column, or press `Cmd/Ctrl+U`. Enter a
universe number between 1 and 63999 and confirm. The app joins that multicast group and starts listening
immediately.

The universe appears as soon as a packet arrives. Manual adds apply to sACN only; ArtNet is not
subscription based.

## ArtNet

The app listens to port 6454 on the selected interface and decodes every ArtDMX packet it can see,
whether the sender used unicast, multicast or broadcast. ArtPollReply packets provide the node's long
name, which then appears as the **Source Name** in the statistics.

No manual step is needed, but the packets must arrive on the selected interface's subnet.

## Reception statistics

Selecting a universe fills the right column with:

- **Universe** and **Universe Hex**: decimal and hexadecimal number.
- **Sender**: IP address of the source.
- **Source Name**: the sACN source name or the ArtNet node's long name, when the source provides one.
- **Status**: **Receiving** while packets arrive, **Timeout** after about 2.5 seconds of silence.
- **Mode**: how the packets are addressed: Unicast, Multicast or Broadcast.
- **FPS**: packets per second, smoothed over recent packets.
- **Priority** and **Sync Address**: sACN only. ArtNet has no equivalent fields.

Below the separator, **Selected Channel** and **Selected Value** follow the channel you last clicked in
the grid.

## Reload the list

**File > Reload Universes** (`Cmd/Ctrl+R`) clears the list. Universes reappear as new packets arrive, or
on the next discovery announcement. It does not change the selected interface or the protocol filter.
