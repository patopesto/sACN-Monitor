## Select a channel

Click any channel to select it. The selected cell gets a light border, and the right column shows its
number under **Selected Channel** and its raw value with a percentage under **Selected Value**.

## View modes

The **Channel View** control switches between three readings of the same data:

- **Channels** shows the channel number, 1 to 512.
- **Values** shows the raw DMX value, 0 to 255.
- **Both** stacks the number above the value.

Use the control in the right column, the **View > Channel View** menu, or the shortcuts:
`Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+C` for channels, `Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+V` for values and `Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+B` for both.

Behind the numbers, a coloured bar fills from the bottom in proportion to the channel's value. The bar
follows the selected theme's accent colour, so activity across the universe is visible at a glance even
in values-only mode.

## Keyboard navigation

With a channel selected, the arrow keys move the selection:

- **Left** and **Right** move one channel at a time.
- **Up** and **Down** move to the channel directly above or below in the current layout, which keeps the
  column when the window is resized.

The same pattern applies to the universe list: `Up` and `Down` move between rows.

## Open a universe in its own window

Right-click a universe and choose **Open in New Window**. The new window opens on that universe with its
protocol already selected, which is handy for putting one universe on a second screen or in a separate
window during a show. **File > New Window** (`Cmd/Ctrl+N`) does the same for an empty window.

## Themes and layout

The theme dots under the settings choose between cyan, blue, purple, red and neutral. The choice applies
to the channel bars, the selected universe row and the controls. Collapse either side column with the
arrows in the DMX header to give the grid more room; the grid reflows and keyboard navigation follows
the new columns.
